- **Labs**: 🎸 **Pulso**, a new prototype in Active projects (`/labs/pulse/`): a one-finger rhythm game to well-known classics played as rock (Himno de la alegría, La Quinta, En la gruta del rey de la montaña, Marcha turca), all in the public domain and recorded by the game's own synthesized band. Notes fall to three pads in an arc at the thumb's reach and are the melody: hit, the guitar sounds; missed, it goes quiet. Some are tapped on the beat, others are held and released exactly when their tail ends. Each song gets faster part by part, with sparks, bouncing pads and fire on a long run, a multiplier, an energy meter that cuts the power when it runs out, a record per song and 🎧 a rhythm tuning for Bluetooth headphones (RP-82, [docs/games/pulse.md](../docs/games/pulse.md)).
