- **Labs**: 🎸 **Pulso**, a new prototype in Active projects (`/labs/pulse/`): a one-finger rhythm game to 90s rock that the game composes itself. Notes fall to three pads in an arc at the thumb's reach: some are tapped on the beat, others are held and released exactly when their tail ends. Nine parts that get faster and harder, a multiplier, an energy meter that cuts the power when it runs out, today's song the same for everyone, a record and 🎧 a rhythm tuning for Bluetooth headphones (RP-82, [docs/games/pulse.md](../docs/games/pulse.md)).
